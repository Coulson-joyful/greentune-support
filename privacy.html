GreenTune · 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

简体中文

更新日期：2026年10月8日

麦克风音频仅在设备上实时处理，用于识别音高。GreenTune 不录制、保存或上传音频。节拍器无需麦克风权限。

语言、乐器、调弦和外观偏好保存在本机。计时信息仅用于打拍测速和节拍调度。

GreenTune 没有开发者运营的服务器、广告、追踪或第三方统计 SDK。购买和恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理，应用通过已验证的交易解锁皮肤，开发者不接收支付卡信息。Apple 按其隐私政策处理自身服务的数据。

你可以在系统设置中撤销麦克风权限，删除应用以移除本地应用数据。设备备份由你的系统设置管理。

主动发送支持邮件时，开发者会收到你提供的信息，仅用于回复和处理问题。请避免发送录音或敏感信息。

开发者及隐私联系邮箱：liuyf21@tsinghua.org.cn

English

Updated: October 8, 2026

Microphone audio is processed on your device in real time to detect pitch. GreenTune does not record, save or upload this audio. Microphone access is not required for the metronome.

Language, instrument, tuning and appearance preferences are stored locally. Timing information is used only for tap tempo and metronome scheduling.

GreenTune has no developer-operated server, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. Purchases and purchase restoration are handled by Apple StoreKit. GreenTune checks verified transactions to unlock skins; the developer does not receive payment card details. Apple handles its own service data under its privacy policy.

You can revoke microphone permission in system settings and remove local app data by deleting the app. Device backups are controlled by your system settings.

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request. Avoid sending recordings or sensitive information.
