GreenTune · 使用帮助 / Support

简体中文

调音：允许麦克风权限，选择乐器和调弦，逐根拨弦。如果没有音高，请检查系统麦克风权限并减少环境噪声。

节拍器：选择 BPM 和拍号，再点开始。连续点击打拍可估算速度。

外观：黑色、白色主题免费。一次性购买解锁乐器主题和受支持设备上的备用图标。默认图标可恢复原始桌面图标。

购买：使用同一 Apple 账号点击恢复购买，可恢复皮肤授权。打赏完全自愿、可重复购买，不解锁功能。商品不可用时，请检查网络并重试。

反馈问题时，请提供应用版本、设备型号、系统版本和复现步骤，可附截图，不要发送支付信息。

English

Tuning: allow microphone access, choose your instrument and tuning, then play one string at a time. If pitch is missing, check microphone permission in system settings and reduce background noise.

Metronome: choose BPM and a time signature, then tap Start. Tap tempo estimates BPM from repeated taps.

Appearance: light and dark themes are free. One purchase unlocks instrument themes and alternate icons on supported devices. Default App Icon restores the original icon.

Purchases: use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account to restore the skin unlock. Tips are optional, repeatable purchases and do not unlock features. If products are unavailable, check your connection and retry.

For support, email your app version, device model, system version and steps to reproduce. Screenshots are optional; do not include payment details.
